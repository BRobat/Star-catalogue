Load your own Gaia query

Run this ADQL in the Gaia Archive (Advanced/ADQL tab), download the result as CSV, then drop it on this page. Columns needed: ra, dec, parallax, phot_g_mean_mag; bp_rp adds colour and pmra, pmdec, radial_velocity add motion.

SELECT TOP 300000 source_id, ra, dec, parallax,
       phot_g_mean_mag, bp_rp,
       pmra, pmdec, radial_velocity
FROM gaiadr3.gaia_source
WHERE parallax > 2
  AND parallax_over_error > 10
  AND bp_rp IS NOT NULL
About this data

Gaia DR3 measured about 1.8 billion sources, far more than a browser can hold. The built-in set is the HYGLike subset of AT-HYG v3.2: 118,658 bright stars with positions from Tycho-2 and Hipparcos, where 98% take their distance and motion from Gaia DR3 parallaxes. The remaining stars, mostly the brightest ones Gaia saturates on, keep Hipparcos distances.

Drift uses each star's Gaia proper motion and, for 84% of stars, its measured radial velocity. Stars without one move only across the sky. Motion is a straight line, which holds up for a few million years; real orbits curve around the Galaxy over hundreds of millions.

Star size reflects how bright each star would look from the camera's current position, computed from its absolute magnitude. Fly close to a star and it brightens.
